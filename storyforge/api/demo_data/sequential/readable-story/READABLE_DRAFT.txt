s01 | reviewed

SCENE 1: FERRY QUAY – DUSK (18:00)

PAGE 1

PANEL 1
Wide shot of the stone ferry quay under cold harbor drizzle. MARA VENN leans against a rusted iron mooring post, her waterproof jacket slick with mist. Beside her stands her brother, IVO VENN, holding a portable field radio unit by its canvas strap. A weathered clock tower behind the slipway shows 18:00.

MARA
Tide is turning, Ivo. Water won’t wait for the archive.

PANEL 2
Close-up on Mara’s open palm. Nestled in her calloused hand are exactly two stamped brass crossing tokens, each marked with a single punch-hole. 

MARA (OFF-PANEL)
Two brass slips. That buys one run out and one run home. Nothing extra.

PANEL 3
Medium shot. Ivo checks the battery dial on the face of the radio unit. Two small green indicator pips glow on the housing.

IVO
Two outgoing signals left on this pack. After that, the board goes cold until the dock recharges it.

PANEL 4
Sera Holt approaches down the wet cobblestone slipway from the street, carrying a heavy, oilskin-wrapped volume tucked under her arm. Her coat is damp from the ten-minute walk.

MARA
You took your time from the archive, Holt.

SERA
Ten minutes at a brisk pace. Walking faster only tears the binding.

PAGE 2

PANEL 1
Close-up on the ledger laid open on the wooden cap of a bollard. Sera’s gloved finger points firmly at an empty, scraped fiber patch in the relief entry row where a date once sat. The ink has been shaved clean away.

SERA
Look here. Not a misprint. A blade scraped the date off this relief log entirely.

PANEL 2
Mara and Ivo lean in close over the open ledger. Mara’s jaw tightens as she examines the thinned, roughened parchment under the harbor lamp.

MARA
Scraped down to the bone. Who touched the book before you took it?

SERA
That is an accusation, not a record. The blade mark shows alteration. It does not name the hands that held the knife.

PANEL 3
Ivo raises the handset to his mouth, thumb pressing the side switch. On the radio chassis, one green pip flickers and clicks out; exactly one green pip remains lit.

IVO
Dock relay to Archive Desk. Page forty-four confirmed damaged. Heading out. 

PAGE 3

PANEL 1
Medium shot. Mara drops one brass token into the dock master’s collection box with a sharp, heavy clink. Her other hand closes tightly around the single remaining token, shoving it deep into her coat pocket.

MARA
One token spent. We’ve got one left to get back.

PANEL 2
Ivo steps back toward the quay shelter, the strap of the half-drained radio over his shoulder. He watches Mara help Sera aboard the tethered wooden ferry.

IVO
I remain on the quay. Keep your eyes on the crossing, Mara.

PANEL 3
Wide shot from the quay. The ferry pushes off from the dock, churning black water in its wake as it heads into the harbor channel toward the distant lights of the council landing. Ivo stands alone on the stone slipway, watching them leave.

s02 | reviewed

PAGE 1

PANEL 1
EXT. COUNCIL LANDING - NIGHT
Wide shot. At 18:20, the wooden ferry grinds against the slick timber pilings of the council landing. Drizzle glistens on the stone quay. Mara loops the wet mooring line around an iron bollard, hauling the boat tight. Sera steps ashore, pinning the oilskin-wrapped ledger tightly against her ribs.

PANEL 2
Medium shot. Mara wipes spray from her eyes. She reaches into her coat, her fingers curling around the cold, single punch-holed brass disc remaining in her pocket.

MARA
Eighteen-twenty. We're at the landing. That transit cost one crossing token, Sera. I’ve got exactly one left.

PANEL 3
Close-up on Sera. Rain runs down her turned-up collar. She secures the wrapped volume with both hands.

SERA
Then we make the trip count. The archive does not hand over its records for empty guessing.

PAGE 2

PANEL 1
Medium shot. Mara and Sera walk toward the iron gates leading to the council chamber stairs. A single wall-mounted lamp casts yellow light across the wet pavement. Mara glares at the volume tucked against Sera's side.

MARA
Ivo radioed page forty-four in to the desk before we left. You showed us the erased date at the quay. Who did it?

PANEL 2
Close-up on Sera’s hands. She pulls back the oilskin flap to reveal the damaged leather binding, keeping her hand clamped on the spine.

SERA
I keep custody of the ledger. And I will not point a finger at anyone.

PANEL 3
Medium shot. Mara stops on the stone landing, turning to face Sera directly.

MARA
We crossed the harbor on one token for an erased date, and you won't name a suspect?

PANEL 4
Close-up on Sera. She pulls the oilskin shut over the ledger binding.

SERA
The scraped paper proves alteration, not who held the blade. I refuse to accuse anyone until we compare this volume against the original receipt.
